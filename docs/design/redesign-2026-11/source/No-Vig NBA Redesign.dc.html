How we read a prop

Two steps: strip the bookmaker margin out of the price, then weigh that fair price against the player’s own numbers.

01

Remove the vig

Books price both sides so their implied probabilities add up to more than 100%. The extra is their margin. Dividing each side by the total gives the fair price.

Over −110
52.38%
Under −110
52.38%
Vig: 104.76% − 100% = 4.76%. Fair price: 50% / 50%.
p = |odds| / (|odds| + 100) // odds < 0
p = 100 / (odds + 100) // odds > 0
p_fair = p / (p_over + p_under)
consensus = mean(p_fair across books)
